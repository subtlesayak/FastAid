## Technical Debt

### Phase 7

- JSON storage is intentionally single-process and host-trusted.
- Managed persistence, backups, encryption at rest, and transactional multi-record writes remain production work.

### Phase 8

- Session and abuse-control state is not shared across replicas.
- Audit append-only behavior is enforced by the application, not a tamper-evident external ledger.

### Phase 9

- Local notifications are simulated; Firebase sender injection and remote receipt are deferred.
- In-process timeouts and haversine ranking must become durable jobs and road/traffic-aware routing.

### Phase 10

- The field UAT guide is complete, but execution requires an HTTPS pilot environment and staged participants.
- Android Gradle deprecation warnings should be cleared before Gradle 10.

## Conclusion

FastAid v1.1 meets its controlled-pilot backend definition of done. It must not be represented as official or production emergency dispatch until the documented persistence, queueing, provider, operations, certification, and field-validation work is completed.
