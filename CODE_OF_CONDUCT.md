# Code of Conduct

FastAid discussions should stay respectful, practical, and safety-aware.

## Expected Behavior

- Be clear and constructive.
- Respect privacy and avoid sharing personal or sensitive emergency details.
- Separate product ideas from operational claims.
- Assume good intent while still reviewing safety-critical claims carefully.
- Prefer evidence from tests, screenshots, logs, or documented behavior.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, or personal attacks
- Publishing private credentials or personal information
- Misrepresenting FastAid as an official emergency dispatch service
- Encouraging unsafe emergency behavior
- Submitting fabricated test evidence or invented field claims

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove content that violates this code. Serious or repeated violations may lead to blocking from project spaces.
